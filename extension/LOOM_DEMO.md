# LiveTask Companion demo flow

1. Open `chrome://extensions` and show the unpacked LiveTask Companion.
2. Open extension settings and briefly show the Supabase/project connection fields without exposing the complete key in the recording.
3. Sign in from the popup.
4. Add a normal task from the popup.
5. Capture the current webpage and show that its source link is stored.
6. Select text on a webpage, right-click it and choose **Save selection to LiveTask**.
7. Open the side panel and show the captured tasks.
8. Start one task, complete another and delete a sample task.
9. Open the Next.js dashboard and show that the extension-created items appear in the same account.
10. Point out the incomplete-task badge, due-today notification setting and keyboard shortcuts.
